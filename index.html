Keep the classic mod cards, give them the new colors

The current management cards put every action in view at once: no menus, no extra clicks, and six mods fit on one screen. That's the part worth keeping.

What dates them is the paint, not the layout. Refreshed keeps the layout, every link, and the density exactly as they are, and only recolors them with the palette the new front page mod tiles already use.

Middle ground goes further toward the new tile prototype: front page image, meta, title, and stats, plus its edit links. The difference is that the edit drawer is always open. No hover, no menus, no icon-only buttons. As on the old cards, the title opens the mod page and the comment and discussion counts open those tabs.

Compact fits four per row: game and category beside the thumbnail, the title under it, and the metrics in the footer. Warnings never add height. They show as small icons on the thumbnail and as notes on the Files and Media links.

Real cards from my own mods. In Middle ground the links go to the real pages and the visibility switch works.

What stays the same

  • Card size, two-column layout, and order of links
  • Every management link visible without opening a menu
  • Counts next to Media, Files, and Articles
  • Visibility radios, stats footer, last comment time

What changes

  • Card and footer surfaces use the front page tile colors
  • Links are neutral and turn orange on hover, so orange stops being wallpaper
  • Counts and section labels are dimmed so link names lead
  • Thumbnail gets rounded corners, title band blends into the card

Small fixes along the way

  • Delete this mod is red, so it no longer looks like every other orange link
  • Pending media gets a small badge instead of hiding inside "(3, 1 pending)"
  • Footer stats stay on one line
  • Content tags sit in the thumbnail's bottom-left corner: red Adult and blue AI (the blue people already know from the AI tags in browse and popular extensions). The footer has a tag count that lights up orange when a tag was added that you haven't seen yet (the tooltip names it), which makes AI tags added by others, missing AI declarations, or tag trolling easy to spot
  • Donation Points status sits next to the visibility switch: green DP when opted in, with an extra split icon when the points are shared with other authors, and gray No DP when not, so a mod you forgot to opt in stands out
  • Comment, discussion, and bug counts turn orange and bold while they have something you haven't seen. In Middle ground, each new item also gets its own orange badge next to the other alerts on the thumbnail (new tag, new bugs, new comments), wrapping to a second row when needed. This builds on the notification read state the site already tracks. Tag changes would need a new notification.

Middle ground: what it keeps from the old cards

  • Every edit section is a visible text link, one click away, with its count
  • Nothing appears only on hover, so it works the same with touch, keyboard, and screen readers
  • Visibility is a labeled switch, not something buried in a menu
  • All text meets WCAG AA contrast on the card, including counts and secondary text
  • Links are grouped in labeled nav regions, and keyboard focus is clearly outlined
